# Asset provenance

The exact retained file hashes are in manifest.json. These assets were copied
without modification into the private UTS kit on 13 November 2026.

- Google Sans v14.000 Regular/Bold/Italic: official upstream
  https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans/releases/tag/v14.000 . SIL OFL in
  fonts/OFL.txt. Retained from Kaggle v0.2.0 at commit
  258aa4ed2ad9ff3a668c6853d1f98a6f338d07ef.
- Google Sans Code Regular/Bold: https://github.com/googlefonts/googlesans-code .
  Licence and conversion/source hashes in fonts/OFL-google-sans-code.txt and
  fonts/code-font-sources.json, retained from the same pinned kit release.
- UTS mark: unchanged original SVG from
  https://www.uts.edu.au/globalassets/resizer/restricted/media/all-pages-sitewide/header/restricteduts-logoheader.svg .
  Retained from the TechFest project's branding record, retrieved there on
  24 October 2026. UTS owns the mark. Identification does not imply endorsement
  or permission for unrelated branding; confirm the consumer's appropriate use.

[UTS guidance](https://educationexpress.uts.edu.au/collections/creating-videos/resources/use-uts-branding-graphics-and-templates/)
identifies NB International/NB Akademie as licensed fonts and Arial as a substitute.
Google Sans is the approved personal kit choice. This is not a corporate template.

No Active Fire photographs, research figures, project results, Kaggle wordmarks
or theme-specific icons are bundled. Consumer projects supply their own images
and retain original credits, licences and any generation prompts. Do not treat
this kit's private status as a licence to redistribute third-party assets.
