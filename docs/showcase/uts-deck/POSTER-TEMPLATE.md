# UTS A1 poster template (portrait and landscape)

Reference build: the Synogize harness poster (`uts-deck/poster/` in the project-15-strategy repository), rendered
with the deck-kit poster engine. Project-specific labels below (subject code, project name) are examples to replace.

**Status: Adopted for review, 17 November 2026.** One standard for Tuan's UTS posters, set from the Active Fire
poster (`deck-kit/projects/fire-occurrence`) and this Synogize poster. Proposed for the deck-kit poster engine; until it
is adopted there, each project applies it in its own spec and CSS.

## Page

| Item | Portrait | Landscape |
|---|---|---|
| Size | A1, 594 × 841 mm | A1, 841 × 594 mm |
| Margin | 18 mm all round | 18 mm all round |
| Grid | 12 columns, 5 mm gap | 12 columns, 5 mm gap; two or three column stacks |
| Output | PDF, plus a 6000-pixel PNG for LinkedIn | same |

## Header

- Top label: "36127 Innovation Lab Capstone | Project 15" (subject | project), 20 pt, caps, no dash before it.
- Title 64 pt, one line where possible; the question 26 pt; team line 22 pt bold. No member names, no QR code.
- **UTS logo:** 32 mm square (the height of the title and question block), unaltered file, at the top margin. Clear space of a quarter of its width (8 mm) on every
  side, kept free of text and colour.
- **Partner logo** (if any): same height as the UTS logo, to its left, with a 1 mm grey divider between them. With no
  partner, the UTS logo sits alone at the top-right. (Active Fire places it top-left beside a masthead; move it to the
  top-right when that poster is next revised.)

## Answer band (directly under the header)

Dark band (#1B2430), three parts: the key number in Viridis yellow (48 pt) with its label; the verdict (28 pt) with
one reason line (22 pt, key terms bold white); NEXT, numbered steps with "Step: detail". The middle part takes the
spare width.

## Sections and cards

- Section labels 20 pt caps with a thin rule; one palette accent per section on the card's top edge.
- Card headings 28 pt, one sentence that states the finding; key terms in the section colour.
- Arm colours are reserved: Terminus 2 / reference blue #375A8C, Custom / ours green #3BBB75 (text #1F7A4F).
- Icons only where they identify something; a step keeps one colour wherever it appears.

## Type

Google Sans; Google Sans Code for code and task names. Seven sizes at A1: title 64, figure 48, subtitle 26, heading 28,
body 22, eyebrow 20, caption 18. Nothing below 18 pt.

## Footer

Left: **project title** bold · team. Right: public link (GitHub profile until a public project repository exists).
